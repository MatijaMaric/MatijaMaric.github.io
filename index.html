About

I studied computing at FER, the University of Zagreb’s Faculty of Electrical Engineering and Computing, and drifted towards machine learning and computer vision. My bachelor’s thesis tracked football players across video frames with OpenCV.

These days the work is less about pixels and more about product. Day to day that means C# on the server and TypeScript in the browser. On my own time I reach for whatever fits the problem, which lately has been Bun, SQLite and Cloudflare Workers.

Away from the keyboard it’s films and series (hence Remindarr), games and music.
